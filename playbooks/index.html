Implementation playbooks

  • Small Business CRM Rollout Playbook

    Roll out a CRM in controlled stages: define the process, reconcile a sample, pilot real use, then cut over with a fallback.

  • SOP Implementation and Rollout Playbook

  • First Operations Manager: A 90-Day Onboarding Plan

    The first operations manager needs authority, context, and a manageable operating rhythm.

  • Second Location Opening: Operations Readiness Playbook

    Opening a second location tests whether the first location’s performance depends on repeatable systems or a few people’s memory.

  • Post-Acquisition Operations Integration Playbook

    After an acquisition, protect current commitments while learning how the business actually runs.

  • Founder-Led Sales Handoff Playbook for a Small Team

    Handing sales to a team requires more than transferring a contact list.

  • Vendor Renewal Calendar for Small Businesses

    Build a vendor renewal calendar that gives owners time to review spending, test alternatives, and act before notice deadlines.

  • Software Subscription Seat Audit Playbook

    Reconcile paid software seats to assigned users and actual use before removing access or changing subscription quantities.

  • Supplier Bid Comparison for Operating Teams

  • Service Backlog Triage Playbook

    Triage a service backlog by age, customer commitment, and blocked work using a daily queue and recovery plan.

  • Weekly Commitment Review for Leadership Teams

  • First-Visit Completion Playbook for Field Service

  • Quote Approval and Delegation Playbook

  • Customer Complaint Escalation Matrix Playbook

  • Shift Handoff Log for Service and Operations Teams

  • Manual Report Retirement Playbook

  • Three-Way Purchase Order Matching Playbook

  • Minimum Stock and Reorder Point Playbook

  • Sales Discount Review and Margin Playbook

  • Rush Order Intake and Capacity Playbook

  • Customer Renewal Risk Handoff Playbook

  • Workflow Pilot Rollback Plan

Choose one operating problem first

Begin with the workflow that is causing missed commitments, repeated approvals, or unreliable reporting. Use the guide to define the decision, the worksheet or example to prepare evidence, and the related service page to understand implementation scope. Test one change before introducing a larger system.

What these resources represent

The worked examples are illustrative and the calculators are planning models. They are not client outcomes or industry benchmarks. Service pages explain potential scope and acceptance criteria; specific responsibilities, timing, and fees are agreed in a written proposal.
